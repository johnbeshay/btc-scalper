Rules that hold regardless

Buy with 8 to 14 minutes leftEarly enough that the outcome is still open, late enough that the price means something.
Stop opening under 3 minutesPrices are nearly settled. Any edge the numbers claim is noise by then.
Avoid anything near 50¢That is where Kalshi's fee peaks, about 3.5% of your stake. A 90¢ contract costs 0.7%.
Avoid long shots under 15¢The fee eats over 6% of your stake, and the model is least reliable out there.
Use limit ordersResting an order costs a quarter of the fee. 100 contracts at 50¢: $0.44 instead of $1.76.
Sell at 85 to 90¢Holding to settlement risks your whole stake to collect the last few cents.
Cut under 20¢There is real money left to recover. Holding to zero recovers nothing.
Sit out under 4¢ of edgeMost windows are priced right. Not trading is usually correct.
